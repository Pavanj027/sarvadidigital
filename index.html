Delivery Approach

A structured process from brief to close-out

The project plan, quality checks and reporting expectations are aligned before fieldwork begins.

Brief & Feasibility

Objectives, audience, geography, sample, languages, timeline and budget.

Plan & Recruit

Field plan, screening criteria, schedules and team briefing.

Execute Fieldwork

Structured data collection, respondent handling and daily progress tracking.

Quality Checks

Agreed validation, backchecks, completeness checks and issue resolution.

Close & Report

Field status, disposition summary and agreed project deliverables.

Project governance is agreed at the outset: roles, milestones, reporting frequency, escalation routes and acceptance criteria.

Quality & Responsibility

Quality checks and respondent care are built into the plan

Specific controls are agreed with each client before fieldwork starts.

Field Quality Controls

  • Clear field brief and questionnaire version control
  • Interviewer briefing and supervision
  • Completeness and logic checks, where applicable
  • Back-checks or validation as agreed
  • Transparent escalation of field issues

Respondent Care & Confidentiality

  • Respectful, voluntary participation
  • Clear explanation of the research activity
  • Consent for recording or observation, where applicable
  • Careful handling of respondent information
  • Confidentiality aligned to client instructions

Data security arrangements, access controls, retention and client compliance protocols are confirmed for each engagement.
